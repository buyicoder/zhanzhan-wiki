In fact, any project must have a front-end backend. The back end determines the real function, the front end determines the external display. For business, the back end is delivery and the front end is marketing.

In the future, marketing becomes important, and everyone learns to market themselves, so the personal knowledge base becomes important.

Some people think that the personal knowledge base is for themselves, but it is not. The personal knowledge base, on the one hand, is for people, it's for business cards, it's for money, and on the other, it's for AI, because it can already work for you, and the more AI knows you, the better it works for you.

Any project should be turned into a front-end project. Any back-end project can actually be shown in a front-end way because the front-end costs have become low.
