AI empowerment must be directed to work, not entertainment.

AI Toys, I don't care unless you're a toy manufacturer, you have your own methodology, channels, platforms, and then you're exploring AI. AI love software, AI electronic pets, pure AI novels, I don't care.

Because the nature of entertainment is human connections. Even for a single game, it is the congenial idea of the game author, which is essentially a spiritual exchange between the author and the player. The author makes the game, the player plays the game, the one-sided output is done; if the player can give back to the author, a closed loop is completed. It's just the same. It's essentially an Internet writer who communicates with his readers. Purely AI-produced web sites have no communication value and naturally no readers.

AI ' s value in writing online helps head writers to write more efficiently, not as a substitute. This essentially enhances the efficiency of writers, not AI's direct entertainment. AI can only increase the efficiency of its work and significantly reduce the amount of time that human beings have to work over time without thinking.

Many people do AI entrepreneurship, always think AI can do anything. In fact, all products used for human entertainment cannot be produced on an AI scale; failure to scale means that it is difficult to commercialize, and it is difficult to earn a lot of money even when they are commercialized, to become workshops.

## AI Three Dividends of Age Entrepreneurship

1. **Tool dividend**: Now entrepreneurs hold AI nuclear weapons faster than ever before. Quick test error, fast market validation, fast R & D iterative.
2. **The track dividend.**The development of AI has led to a number of new tracks, such as the transformation of the company AI, personal AI literacy. Note the difference between corporate AI transition and tool dividend: You can use the AI tool to do business AI transformation service, one tool and one track.
3. **Flow dividend**:Ai carries traffic. Now any AI topic, without your authority and your knowledge of the Internet, can easily be accessed.

And that's why the most lucrative AI entrepreneurs you've ever seen are all the students who sell classes.

## A judgement for an entrepreneur.

I'm still in need of capacity and ecological position. If you're already doing something on a traditional track, the best thing to do is use AI to optimize your own work flow, not to abandon the existing track. And if you have an AI nuclear weapon, don't think you can challenge any track: you should go to the new track that has emerged as a result of the development of AI, not to the traditional track. All they have to do is follow the times, and then there's no chance. Now, for example, big Internet factories, even if you can recode their software with AI at one tenth of the cost, you can't compete with them, because they're already at the ecological level.
