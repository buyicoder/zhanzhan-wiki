AI work system solves the question of "Agent says it's done, whether reality is really done." Objectives, competencies, evidence, inspections and rollbacks are more important than opening several more models.

## It's in the whole system.

Inputs, abilities and tools are used here to synthesize workflows: people are responsible for problem-setting and condensing, Agent is responsible for investigation and execution, masters and managers manage the context, and Maker and Checker are separately validated.
