The input system does not solve " where to collect more links " , but rather how to reach out to facts, needs, people and stories before sending them into judgement and action.

## It's in the whole system.

This is the first stop of the power cycle: reality issues determine what I'm looking for, official files, GitHub, papers and real business provide evidence, popular culture helps me understand people, AI helps me start the problem. The input must eventually return to work, project or decision-making.
