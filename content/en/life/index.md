Life Operating Systems address the problem of “increasing capacity and output while people are being depleted”. Work is only a part of life and efficiency cannot be a new virtue.

## It's in the whole system.

Inputs, projects and expressions ultimately serve real life. The ability to sleep, space, rhythm, relationships, cash flow and stop working determines whether the system will operate in the long term.
