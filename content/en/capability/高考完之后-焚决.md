## Step two: build your data assets.

**5. Let AI teach you Git and GitHub.**Those outside the threshold do not feel obliged to learn, and those within the threshold feel extremely important. Only programmers used them in the past, and now AI has lowered the use threshold to zero. GitHub, which is equivalent to a worldwide schoolboy putting his homework on the web site for you to copy; there's no time for troublemakers to write it, you write it for him, and you have your name on it. Your GitHub project is your time and your business card.

**6. Daily summary journals.**It's a matter of heart, too. It's not important to record anything, it's important to start recording. **Systematization of personal data.**Use AI to organize journals, notes, project documents into a searchable knowledge base. Core concepts: embedding, vector database, RAG. There is no need to be an expert, but to explain what they are.

**Building personal work streams through AI.**Every day, learning progress is reported, the knowledge base is updated, and daily learning and work accumulates into reusable assets.
