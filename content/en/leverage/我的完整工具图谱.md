## Methodology and agreement

These are not software names, but they affect delivery quality more than a change in software.

- **Target success criteria Investigation plan Implementation testing feedback deposition**: Define the end condition and avoid Agent replacing the result with a nice report.
- **Master, project manager.**: I'm only going to control the target, and I'm going to hold the project boundary and report back; I'm not going to let a group of Agent make a scene in a group chat.
- **Maker + Checker**: The achiever cannot use his/her own automation as a substitute for independent acceptance, and the key vision is to see the true screenshot.
- **Worktree / Step / Rollback Point**: Parallel tasks are segregated, changes are submitted to range, and when failed, the last available status is returned.
- **GitHub Helipad collaboration**: Task packages, diff, tests, screenshots, reports and responses are all searchable products and do not depend on the oral context in the chat window.

## My final judgment.

Full-scale does not amount to a stack of names. My default mill is responsible for getting people to start, and the complete map is responsible for explaining how I switch when I encounter a different task; the technology vault is responsible for running the product, and the methods and protocols are responsible for making it credible.

If you haven't run through a full production chain, don't go through the page at once. I'll do what I want, and then I'll do what I want; when you're really constrained, the alternative means will make sense.
