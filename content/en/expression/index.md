Content and expression address issues that are “experienced, judged but not understood”. I don't want to create anxiety for traffic, I don't want AI to grind words into standard answers without character.

## It's in the whole system.

The expression translates project experience into public knowledge and also sends feedback back to the system. General cultural literacy helps me understand shared narratives, and AI can review them, but the first person says I have to take the judgement.
