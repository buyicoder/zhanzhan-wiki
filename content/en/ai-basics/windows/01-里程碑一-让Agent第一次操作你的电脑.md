## Before you do it.

Some say AI doesn't need to learn. But someone's always thinking, "I'm about to be abandoned by the AI age -- watching others use AI skilledly, but not myself, panicking."

This class is built for you by the confidence that "I will never fall again, no matter how fast I run." After this lesson, AI will be included in your cognitive context: it will develop and not escape your understanding framework. You'll tell who's selling anxiety and who's passing knowledge.

That's why AI is particularly good at reading and writing Markdown.
