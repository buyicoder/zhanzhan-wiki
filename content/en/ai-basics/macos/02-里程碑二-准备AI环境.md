## What's the problem with this?

After the first time Agent was allowed to operate the computer, the real obstacle was not “no questions”, but the tool chain was unstable: It's working today, and tomorrow's login fails; it's loaded with a lot of tools without knowing who's responsible for what. This is about creating a sustainable environment rather than collecting software.

## What will you finish?

- Distinguisheds the daily dialogue tool, Agent in the editor, and the execution type of Agent to operate the project.
- Establish a combination of at least daily access and complex implementation portals.
- Know the level at which the account number, network, model access or terminal should be checked when encountered.

## Criteria for success

You can steadily open at least one daily AI tool and one executive Agent on your Mac and validate the division of labour with the same small task; once closed and reopened, you can continue working.

## Capacity migration

You don't have to reset your software list. You'll judge whether the mission needs an answer or an "execution", then check the three-tier access, access and work catalogue conditions.
